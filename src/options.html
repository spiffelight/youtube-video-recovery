Archive access

Required. Firefox does not grant this at install time.

Without this, no archived title, date or thumbnail can be fetched and every lookup comes back empty. Chrome grants this automatically; Firefox requires it to be granted once.

Local lookups

Optional. Everything else works without these.

If you watched the video before it went away, your history still has its title — instantly, with no network request. It also proves the video existed, which tells a deleted video apart from an ID that never existed.

A bookmark saved while the video was alive keeps its original title.

Stored data

Kept in the extension's own local storage, on this machine only.

Results are cached so a video you have already looked up answers instantly. Found records are kept 30 days, misses 6 hours, and expired entries are swept automatically. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Only the 11-character video ID of the page you are looking at is used as the search term, and matches are used only to fill in that panel. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
